# Post-benchmark comparator amendment, 2026-10-01

The nine-model benchmark was completed before this amendment. RF had DFC MAE .2997 versus .3440 for the prior gene ElasticNet, but the CI for this contrast included zero. RF versus the quality Ridge had improvement .0786, CI [.0111,.1447]. It is necessary to distinguish RNA contribution from changing the regression learner; this amendment adds quality-only RF and metadata-only RF with exactly the same tree/leaf grids and donor splits. This is a comparability check prompted by observed outcomes, not an independently prespecified confirmation. All three fixed targets/seeds remain reported.

Two quality/metadata baselines x three targets x three seeds x five folds = 90 outer models, 900 total fits. Inner imputation/scaling only on training data. Gene RF predictions are unchanged. Compare paired errors against the two new RF baselines and report percentile CI, unadjusted exploratory P and Holm over the six baseline x endpoint contrasts. Do not claim independent model superiority from any of these tests.

Also decompose existing errors into fixed observed-AT8 categories on DFC: <0.1%, 0.1 to <1%, and >=1%. These categories are descriptive evaluation strata; never used for selecting a model. Report counts and errors, do not average categories to replace the primary population MAE. Compute every donor's contribution to squared-error improvement relative to quality RF and recompute R2 after excluding the single largest measured DFC AT8 (diagnostic only, model not refit, explicit post hoc). This tests domination by one high-burden donor.
